Privacy Policy — ShikshaNova Foundation

Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

ShikshaNova Foundation ("we", "us", "our") respects the privacy of everyone who uses our website at shikshanovafoundation.org. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and how we keep it secure.

Information we collect

When you complete our contact, volunteer or newsletter form, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, location and message. We only collect information that you choose to provide to us.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to respond to your message, to process your volunteer or internship application, to send you updates about our programmes and events, and to stay in contact with you. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Internship applications

If you apply for our internship programme, we collect your name, email, phone number, college, course, location and your reply to our questions. We use them only to review your application, contact you and, for selected interns, run the programme and issue certificates. We keep them only as long as needed for these purposes. Applicants must be 18 or above. To ask for your details to be corrected or deleted, write to info@shikshanovafoundation.org.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies that are necessary for basic functionality, and it stores your cookie preference in your browser. We may also use analytics tools such as Google Analytics, which collect anonymous usage data, to help us improve the site.

Donations and payments

If you make a donation through our website, payment processing is handled securely by a third-party payment gateway such as Razorpay or Instamojo. We do not store your card or bank details on our servers.

Children's information

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. Where our programmes involve students, information is collected through their school or institution with the appropriate permissions, and photographs are published only where consent has been given.

Data security

We use appropriate technical measures to protect your information. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to your information, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Please use our contact page to do so.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For any question relating to this policy, please get in touch through our contact page.
