Our Programmes — ShikshaNova Foundation

From the classroom to the first job — work that changes outcomes.

Core programmes

The programmes at the heart of our work with schools, colleges and students.

Life skills curriculum, classes 6–8

A complete, classroom-ready curriculum: lesson plans, a teacher's guide, student workbooks and activity books, all written specifically for this age group.

School partnerships and MOUs

We sign memoranda of understanding with local schools so that the curriculum reaches classrooms directly and benefits as many students as possible.

Internship programme

Structured internship opportunities that give young people real-world experience and prepare them for the workplace. Coordinated from our Bihar office at Nokha, Rohtas. Free for students of partner colleges.

Free college internship: details and apply

Job placement programme

Placement support that connects young people to suitable job opportunities once their training is complete.

Campus career-readiness workshops

Sessions delivered on college campuses covering communication, interview preparation, workplace behaviour and the basics of digital and AI tools.

Teacher training

Working with teachers on classroom practice and on delivering the life skills curriculum confidently.

In the pipeline

The next steps in our expansion.

Nationwide expansion

With our area of operation covering all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh and all 38 districts of Bihar, we are now planning expansion into further states.

Skill development course with direct placement, Rohtas, Bihar

A skill training course launching in the Nokha area of Rohtas district, Bihar, where practical training is followed immediately by direct job placement.

Online skills and certification programme

An online programme giving students and young people practical training in digital skills, AI, communication, career readiness, online work and entrepreneurship. Participants receive a certificate on completion.

How a programme reaches a student

The same four steps, whether the work happens in a village school or on a college campus.

1. Partner

We sign an MOU with a school, college or local institution.

2. Train

Our facilitators deliver sessions, and teachers are trained to continue them.

3. Practise

Students apply the learning through activities, workbooks and internships.

4. Place

Eligible students receive placement support into work or further training.
