ShikshaNova Foundation — Education, Skills and Opportunity for Every Learner in India

ShikshaNova Foundation works with schools, colleges and young people to deliver life skills, employability training and AI awareness — so that every learner gets a fair chance to grow. Our area of operation covers all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh and all 38 districts of Bihar, with a long-term goal of serving learners across India.

Our mission

To put practical, high-quality learning within reach of every student we work with — and to make sure that learning leads somewhere: to a skill, an internship or a job.

Teach what schools often cannot

Life skills, communication, digital and AI literacy, and an understanding of how work actually happens.

Connect learning to work

Internships and placement support so that training ends in a real opportunity, not a certificate alone.

Reach where support is thin

We work district by district, with a focus on rural schools and colleges that sit outside the usual networks.

What we focus on

From primary classrooms to college campuses, we work across the education chain — with students, teachers and institutions.

Education and literacy

Taking foundational learning and literacy support to every student, from Class 6 onward.

Life skills

A practical life skills curriculum designed for classes 6 to 8.

Employability

Preparing young people for internships, interviews and their first job.

AI and digital awareness

Giving students and teachers a working understanding of AI and digital tools.

Entrepreneurship

Building the mindset and skills to start a small enterprise.

Teacher development

Training teachers and strengthening classroom practice.

Our work on the ground

Training sessions, campus workshops and career-readiness programmes of the kind we deliver with students in Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.
