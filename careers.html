Careers — ShikshaNova Foundation

Build your career while building someone else's.

Why work here

This is early-stage work in districts where education support is thin. The responsibility is real, the feedback is immediate, and what you build stays in use.

Work that is visible

You will see the effect of what you do in the room, in the same week.

Room to build

Programmes here are still being designed. Good ideas get tried rather than filed.

Fair and open hiring

We recruit on merit, and we do not discriminate on caste, religion, gender, disability or background.

Current openings

To apply, send your CV and a short note about the role to info@shikshanovafoundation.org, or use the contact form and mention the role name.

Programme Coordinator

Runs school partnerships end to end: signing MOUs, scheduling sessions, coordinating facilitators and reporting on delivery.

  • Location: Deoria, Uttar Pradesh
  • Type: Full time
  • Experience: 1–3 years
  • Qualification: Graduate
Apply

Training Facilitator (Life Skills)

Delivers life skills and career-readiness sessions in schools and colleges, and supports teachers in using our curriculum.

  • Location: Uttar Pradesh and Bihar — field based
  • Type: Full time
  • Experience: 0–2 years
  • Qualification: Graduate
Apply

Placement and Employer Relations Officer

Builds relationships with employers, matches trained students to openings, and tracks placement outcomes.

  • Location: Nokha, Rohtas, Bihar
  • Type: Full time
  • Experience: 2–4 years
  • Qualification: Graduate
Apply

Curriculum Associate

Writes and edits lesson plans, workbooks and teacher guides for classes 6 to 8, and for our online certification programme.

  • Location: Remote or Deoria
  • Type: Full time
  • Experience: 1–3 years
  • Qualification: Postgraduate preferred
Apply

Internships

Our internship programme is coordinated from our Bihar office. Address: In front of Car Parking, Behind SBI Bank & Pathak Complex, Nokha, District Rohtas, Bihar – 802215. Internships run across programme delivery, curriculum, communications and placement support, and are open to students and recent graduates.

Interns receive a structured brief, a supervisor, and a certificate on successful completion.

Apply for an internship

Volunteering

If you can give a few hours a week rather than full time, volunteering is the better fit. Volunteers teach sessions, mentor students, help with translation and content, or support events in their own district.
