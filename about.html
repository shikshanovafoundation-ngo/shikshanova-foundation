About ShikshaNova Foundation

The story, vision and legal foundation of ShikshaNova Foundation.

Our story

ShikshaNova Foundation began with a simple conviction: education should not stop at textbooks. It should also build life skills, employability and an understanding of the technologies shaping the world, including artificial intelligence.

We started in Deoria, Uttar Pradesh, and our area of operation covers all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh and all 38 districts of Bihar, with a long-term goal of operating nationally.

As a Section 8 company, we operate on a fully not-for-profit basis. Every rupee and every hour of effort goes directly into the future of the children and young people we serve.

Our vision

To establish ShikshaNova Foundation among India's leading education organisations — delivering a national-level curriculum, AI education, entrepreneurship programmes, student placement, teacher training and skill development as one connected system.

Our mission

To put practical, high-quality learning within reach of every student we work with, and to make sure that learning leads somewhere — to a skill, an internship or a job.

The values we work by

These are the standards we hold ourselves to in every classroom and every partnership.

Dignity

Every student is treated as capable. We teach to strengths, not to deficits.

Usefulness

If a lesson does not help a student in real life or at work, it does not belong in our curriculum.

Transparency

Our registrations, governance and reports are published openly on this website.

Reach

We go to the districts and schools that are usually left out, not only the easy ones.

Sectors we work in

These are the areas we work in. Our area of operation covers all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh and all 38 districts of Bihar.

Children & Youth

Supporting the all-round development of children and young people, from Class 6 through to postgraduate level.

Education and literacy

Strengthening foundational learning and literacy.

Rural development

Widening opportunity at the village level and tackling poverty.

Vocational training

Hands-on training tied to real work.

Skill development

Raising the employability of young people.

Women and marginalised groups

Training designed to widen access for those who need it most.

Our objects

Under our Memorandum of Association, we pursue the following objectives across India, entirely on a not-for-profit basis.

Education for underprivileged children

Creating learning opportunities for children from economically weaker backgrounds.

Welfare of children in need

Working for the wellbeing of poor and vulnerable children.

Awareness programmes

Organising awareness campaigns and workshops in the communities we serve.

Skill development and vocational training

Training for young people, women and marginalised groups.

    Our journey so far

    Key milestones in the registration and approval of the Foundation.

    23 April 2026

    ShikshaNova Foundation is incorporated as a Section 8 company under the Companies Act, 2013.

    11 May 2026

    Form 104, the application for provisional 12A registration and 80G approval, is filed on the Income Tax e-filing portal.

    18 May 2026

    Provisional 12A registration under Section 332 and provisional 80G approval under Section 354 are granted, both valid from Tax Year 2026–27 to 2028–29.

    2026 onwards

    Programme delivery begins across Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, alongside the opening of our Bihar office at Nokha, Rohtas.
