Frequently asked questions

How can my school or college work with ShikshaNova Foundation?

Write to us with your institution's name, location and the classes you would like covered. We will share the curriculum outline and, if it is a fit, prepare an MOU. There is no fee charged to the school for our core programmes.

Are donations eligible for tax benefit?

Yes. We hold provisional 80G approval under Section 354 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, valid from Tax Year 2026–27 to 2028–29, and a receipt with 80G certificate is issued for every donation.

How do I apply for an internship?

Use the contact form and select "Internship", or email us with your CV. Our internship programme is coordinated from our Bihar office at Nokha, Rohtas.

Do students pay for your training?

Our school and college programmes are delivered free to students. Where a specialised skill course carries a cost, it is stated clearly before enrolment.

Can I volunteer if I live outside Uttar Pradesh and Bihar?

Yes. Content, curriculum, translation and online mentoring can all be done remotely. Tell us what you can offer on the volunteer form.

Where can I see your registration documents?

All registrations and their numbers are listed on our transparency page, and copies are available on request.
