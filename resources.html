Our policies

Non-discrimination

We admit students and recruit staff without discrimination on caste, religion, gender, disability, or social and economic background.

Safeguarding & child protection

Everyone working with students, minors or adults, follows our conduct rules, and sessions with minors are never conducted without a school representative present.

Privacy

We collect only the information people give us, and we never sell or rent it. See our privacy policy.
